Summarize a paper given a DOI, URL, or pasted abstract. Provides structured extraction (Motivation, Approach, Key Findings, Strengths, Limitations, Relevance) without full wiki compilation.

Usage: `/summarize <DOI or URL>`

Invoke `paper-distill:paper-distill`.
